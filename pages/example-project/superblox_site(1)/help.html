How do I play?

Click the Play Now button on any game to join. You will need the SuperBlox player installed.
How do I build?

Download SuperBlox Studio from the Create page and start building your own places.
What are Tickets?

Tickets (Tix) are the free currency earned by visiting places daily. Robux is the premium currency.
